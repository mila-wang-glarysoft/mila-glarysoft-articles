Glary Utilities 6.43 is not a big feature release, and it should not be treated like one. It is a small maintenance update for users who already rely on Glary Utilities for regular PC cleanup and optimization.

## What Changed

The update includes a faster clipboard cleaning algorithm in Memory Defrager, an improved free-space wiping algorithm in Wipe Free Space, minor GUI improvements, and minor bug fixes.

## Why It Still Matters

Small utility updates matter because they keep the tools people already use feeling steady. A few seconds saved here, a small interface cleanup there, and fewer small bugs all add up over repeated maintenance sessions.

## No Need To Overthink It

If Glary Utilities is already part of your routine, this is the kind of update you install and continue working as usual. It does not change the way you use the app.

## Where To Look

After updating, check Memory Defrager and Wipe Free Space if those tools are part of your workflow. Otherwise, run your normal scan, review the results, and keep your usual maintenance rhythm.
