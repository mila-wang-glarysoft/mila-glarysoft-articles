1\. **Why didn’t I receive the license code?**

If you haven't received the license code, there could be several reasons:

1) Check your Spam folder: Your email settings may have filtered the email containing the license code as spam. Please check your Spam folder to see if it's there.

2) Verify your email address: Double-check if you provided the correct and valid email address when you placed the software order. An incorrect email address could result in not receiving the license code.

3) Email service provider restrictions: Some email service providers have strict filters that may reject emails from unknown senders. Ensure your email service provider allows emails from Glarysoft or add their email address to your safe senders list.

If you have checked these possibilities and still cannot find the email with the license code, please get in touch with us at [support@glarysoft.com](mailto:support@glarysoft.com), and we will assist you promptly.  
  
  
  
  
**2\. Lost your license?**

If you have lost your license code, please visit the [Lost License](https://www.glarysoft.com/support/lost-license/) or [My Account](https://my.glarysoft.com/) page to retrieve it. Please ensure that the email address you use to retrieve the license code is the same one you used when you originally ordered the product.

Alternatively, you can email [support@glarysoft.com](mailto:support@glarysoft.com) and provide your name, email address, and order number (if known). We will be happy to assist you in finding your license code.
