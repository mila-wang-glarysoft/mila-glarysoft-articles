If you have lost files from external devices such as external hard drives, memory cards, SD cards, digital cameras, USB flash drives, etc., Glarysoft File Recovery can assist you in recovering them.

However, there is an additional step for external devices: they must be connected to your computer and recognized or detected. Once you have ensured this, follow the same steps for file recovery.
