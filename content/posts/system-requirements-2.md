Glarysoft software is compatible with various versions of Windows and supports the following releases: **Windows 11, 10, 8, 8.1, 7, Vista, and XP, both 32-bit and 64-bit versions**.

**Minimal System Configuration**:

- Graphics mode: 800×600 with 16-bit colors

- At least 20 MB of free hard disk space

- Intel Pentium or AMD K5 processor with 133 MHz

- 32 MB of RAM

**Recommended System Configuration**:

- Graphics mode: 1024×768 with 32-bit color

- 30 MB or more of free hard disk space

- Intel Pentium 3/4 or AMD Athlon processor with 500 MHz or above

- 128 MB of RAM or more

- Internet access

Please ensure that your computer meets these requirements to ensure optimal performance when using Glary software.
